Delete your account or data

This page is for the Untangled Knitting app by Lilac Elephants Ltd.

Delete your account in the app

Open Untangled, tap the three dots in the top right of the home screen, then Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data: your profile, uploaded pattern PDFs, parsed walkthroughs, projects, progress, row counters, notes, and highlights. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Delete your account by email

If you can't access the app, email support@untangledknitting.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account" and we'll delete it within 7 days.

Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual projects, patterns, and notes in the app at any time — deleted items are removed from our servers immediately. To request deletion of specific data without closing your account, email support@untangledknitting.com.

What we retain

Parse-usage records (how many pattern parses your account has used) are retained while your account exists, to operate plan limits and billing fairly. They're deleted with your account. Anonymised analytics that can't be linked back to you may be retained.

Questions: support@untangledknitting.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
